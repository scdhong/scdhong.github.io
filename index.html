Research

I study how robots can perceive people’s emotions and intentions, and communicate their own, so that working together feels natural. Both papers are first-author work.

“I’m Not Mad, Just Focused”: Understanding Human Emotions in Human-Robot Collaboration

Seung Chan Hong, Dana Kulić, Leimin Tian

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 2026. To be presented at ICRA 2027.

A vision-language model reads emotion from the whole scene rather than the face alone, and matches human observers better than a CNN baseline (0.860 vs. 0.771 semantic similarity). On a service robot that adapts its apology after a failure, 31 of 40 study participants preferred the emotion-aware robot.

“One Soy Latte for Daniel”: Visual and Movement Communication of Intention from a Robot Waiter to a Group of Customers

Seung Chan Hong, Leimin Tian, Akansel Cosgun, Dana Kulić

IEEE RO-MAN, 2024. Oral presentation.

Restaurant robots usually stop at one spot per table. Stopping at each customer and showing their order on screen raised group delivery accuracy from 37% to 87%, and was the condition participants preferred most.

Quantum Machine Learning

Monash DeepNeuron, 2025. Founder and team lead.

Started the student team’s first quantum AI project and led it over two semesters. We built simulated quantum models in PennyLane: a quantum-kernel SVM, a quantum CNN matching a classical CNN baseline on MNIST, and a quantum GAN that generates handwritten digits.

With Min Hao, Nathan Wong, Sanket Shivashankaran and Daniel Arnould.
